## Review Response (13-REVIEWS.md, Antigravity lane)

**R-02 [MEDIUM] — "Plan 03 Task 1's single-pass `rumdl check --fix` across ~471 violations /
200+ files is a wide diff; automated formatting could inadvertently touch YAML frontmatter or
tables parsed by `sync.py`'s regexes, beyond what the planned spot-check would catch. Suggested
fix: after the bulk fix, run `sync.py` (or an equivalent quick check) across all touched
`.planning` files."** — **Incorporated, in a cheaper and stricter form than the suggestion.**
Running `sync.py` itself over the tree is not viable as a check: its parsing subcommands
(`create-issues`, `beads-recall`, `close-wave`) all mutate a `bd` database or require `bd` on
PATH, so a post-fix sweep would either be a no-op or a write. The equivalent-check branch of the
suggestion is what this plan takes: Task 1 now brackets the fixer with a before/after multiset of
the exact structural tokens `sync.py`'s six module-level regexes anchor on
(`TASK_RE`, `NAME_RE`, `BEADS_ID_RE`, `FRONTMATTER_RE`, `BEADS_EPIC_RE`, `DEPENDS_ON_RE`), diffed
for exact equality. Since `--fix` cannot insert any of those tokens, exact equality is a sufficient
proof that none was deleted or mangled — and it is one `grep | sort | uniq -c` per side rather
than a bd-dependent parse sweep. A `verify.plan-structure` `task_count` comparison on the three
in-flight Phase 13 plans backs it up. Recorded as threat T-13-16, as a `must_haves.truths` entry,
in Task 1's `<action>` Steps A/C, in its `<verify><automated>`, and in three acceptance criteria.
The check's ceiling (repo-wide total, not per-file) is stated in the action rather than hidden.
