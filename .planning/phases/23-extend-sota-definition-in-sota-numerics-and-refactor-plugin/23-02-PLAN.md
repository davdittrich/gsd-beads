## Alternatives Considered

- **Prune the fragment's restatement of checker internals**: the planner learns the mechanics from the gate's own failure output instead of from the prompt. The script prints one `<plan_path>: <reason>` line per violation naming the exact failing condition and exactly one `remediation: fix the plans above, then re-run /gsd-plan-phase <phase> --force` line, at `` `.gsd/capabilities/sota-numerics/scripts/check-alternatives.py:323` `` (read directly and verified 2026-09-06). Every deleted clause is reproduced there at the moment it matters, and only then.
- **Grow the fragment and let the total rise past 45 lines**: gsd-core enforces no line, token, or KB ceiling on fragments below an 8 MiB DoS backstop (`` `/home/dd/.claude/gsd-core/bin/lib/capability-validator.cjs:2672-2724` ``, verified 2026-09-06), so nothing mechanical would stop it. Rejected because the cost is paid model-side, not host-side: [Anthropic, "Effective context engineering for AI agents" (2025)](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents) frames context as a finite resource whose quality degrades as it grows, and [Chroma Research, "Context Rot" (2025)](https://www.morphllm.com/context-rot) measured that degradation across all 18 models tested, monotonically with input length and independent of whether the window is nominally full.
- **Move the planner detail into README.md and point the fragment at it**: the fragment shrinks to a pointer and the rules live once, in the README. Rejected on correctness before cost: gsd-core materializes `fragment.inline` into the prompt verbatim with nothing that reads a sibling file (`` `/home/dd/.claude/gsd-core/references/loop-hook-dispatch.md` ``, verified 2026-09-06), so the planner would receive a pointer to a file it has no guaranteed access to, and the meaning would then live in two places that drift.

Decided by: performance. The quantity being minimized is per-invocation prompt tokens on the planner's context, the highest-ranked criterion, and pruning restatement is the only one of the three options that reduces it without deleting a rule or introducing an unresolvable pointer.

### Internal design alternatives

- **Prune before adding versus add before pruning**: prune first (Task 1, landing at exactly 10 lines) so the budget headroom is proven before it is spent. Adding first would leave the fragment temporarily over its own ceiling and make a failed prune look like a budget failure rather than a rule-loss failure.
- **Merging the three pairing lines versus deleting two of them**: merge, not delete. NOTES.md §5 records that this fragment and `TestFoundationalCitationPairing` teach one rule from two seats and must stay in step; deleting the canonical-pairing guidance would let a foundational citation fail a gate the test says should pass it.
