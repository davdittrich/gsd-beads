`beads-status` is runnable on demand (B13), printing the plan-task <-> bd issue mapping for a phase,
including orphans on both sides: a bd issue under the epic that matches no current plan task, and a
plan task carrying no `<beads-id>` at all. Today `beads-status` only fires via lifecycle

human-invoked branch (D-07).

lifecycle step to regenerate it, and surfaces drift (either direction) that would otherwise only
appear inside `BEADS.md`'s frontmatter counts.

Step 2e in `beads-status/SKILL.md`, and a `TestOnDemandStatus` test suite.
